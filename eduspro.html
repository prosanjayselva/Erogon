EduSPro

Educational Support For Students Who Need It Most

EduSPro is an educational support programme that reaches young students at school level who are in the most difficult situations of their lives, especially children without parents or with single parents in absolute poverty.

Programme target: raise ₹1,00,000 from sponsors for each child's education support.

What EduSPro Does

The programme identifies deserving students, connects them with compassionate sponsors, and helps sustain their educational journey with continuity and dignity.

Why It Matters

Many bright students face severe financial hardship. EduSPro exists to prevent education from stopping when a child needs support the most.
