Career Opportunities

ERGON Foundation supports employers and job seekers by connecting the right talent with the right opportunities based on skills, qualifications and expectations.

For Job Seekers

  • Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender
  • Educational Qualification and Address
  • Contact Number and Email ID
  • Years of Experience and Skills
  • Preferred Job Role, Industry and Location
  • Current CTC, Expected CTC and Notice Period
  • Languages Known and Resume Upload

For Employers

  • Organisation Name, Contact Person and Designation
  • Industry Type and Job Role Required
  • Number of Vacancies and Qualification Required
  • Experience Required and Salary Range
  • Job Location and Employment Type
  • Job Description Upload and Additional Expectations
