About ERGON

Building Welfare For People, Pets, and the Planet

The vision of ERGON Foundation is to work for the welfare of People, Pets, and the Planet through charitable and sustainable initiatives. The Foundation is committed to supporting poor and needy communities, caring for animals, and protecting the environment in Tamil Nadu and across India.

Registered charitable trust focused on measurable, community-led impact.

Our Vision

We work for the welfare of People, Pets, and the Planet through thoughtful, transparent and sustainable action. Every programme is designed to build dignity, care and long-term resilience.

How We Work

  • Grassroots engagement with real local needs
  • Transparent use of donations and partnerships
  • Programmes that combine compassion with continuity
